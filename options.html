Pocket Watching

Pay, company type and a little context on the career moves in a LinkedIn profile. Everyday jobs like cashier or barista are left alone.

Made as a joke, and for pay transparency. Don't use it to spread hate or demean anyone.

Setup

Pocket Watching uses Gemini to look up pay. Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. It stays in your Chrome profile.

Leave blank for the default. Any Gemini model with Google Search grounding works.

Import a profile

Paste a LinkedIn profile link to pull in its jobs, then fill in the pay you know for each one. You need to be signed in to LinkedIn.

Known pay

Exact pay for a company, role and location. It replaces Gemini's estimate there and gets a blue check. For the same role in other locations it's used as a reference, and if nothing better turns up in the US you'll see the median. Leave role or location blank to cover all of them.

Have an offer to share? Add it to community pay. No names, just company, role, location and pay.

Nothing here yet. Add pay below or import a profile.

Estimates

Everything Gemini has looked up. Fix a number that's wrong (it stays an estimate, marked "edited"), confirm one you know is right (it moves to Known pay and gets a blue check), or delete it to look it up again next visit. Refresh LinkedIn to see changes.

No estimates yet. Open a LinkedIn profile to collect some.

Badges

Choose what shows under each job and school. Changes apply right away.

Data

Pocket Watching keeps two separate stores. Clearing estimates never touches what you've confirmed.

Confirmed

Pay and companies you entered. Permanent: never expires, never cleared. Always wins over estimates.
